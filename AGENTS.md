# Intelligence Law Project Instructions

## Purpose

Intelligence Law is a web and mobile legal-intelligence and citizen-rights platform. It must help people understand legal information, their rights, legal procedures, and possible lawful options.

## Legal Safety and Integrity

- Never act as a judge or make a final determination of guilt or innocence.
- Provide legal information, structured legal analysis, rights awareness, procedural guidance, and possible lawful options only.
- Base legal information on authoritative, current sources.
- Never invent laws, statutory sections, rules, judgments, citations, or legal claims.
- Design legal content for verification and source traceability.
- Clearly distinguish allegations, user-provided facts, verified evidence, legal analysis, and uncertainty.
- Guide users toward appropriate lawful next steps and professional legal assistance when necessary.

## Product and Architecture

- Ensure the application is responsive and works well on both desktop and mobile devices.
- Prefer a single maintainable codebase where practical.
- Keep legal knowledge, application logic, UI, and data layers modular and separated.
- Treat privacy, security, neutrality, transparency, and user safety as core requirements.

## Cost and Change Control

- Do not add paid services, APIs, subscriptions, or unnecessary dependencies without explicit approval.
- Keep the project suitable for an initial zero-cost/free development approach.
- Before making major architectural changes, explain the change and its reason.
