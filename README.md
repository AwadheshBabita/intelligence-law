# Intelligence Law

## AI-Powered Legal Intelligence & Citizen Rights Platform

Intelligence Law is a legal technology project designed to help people understand Indian laws, legal rights, legal procedures, and possible legal remedies in simple language.

### Our Vision

AI will not act as a judge or replace the court.

AI will help people understand:

- What the law says
- What their legal rights are
- What powers an authority has
- What the limits of those powers are
- Which laws or sections may be relevant
- What facts and evidence may be important
- What lawful steps may be available
- When professional legal assistance may be necessary

### Core Principles

- Law-first
- Evidence-aware
- Rights-focused
- Neutral and fair
- Transparent
- Source-based
- Privacy-conscious
- Human decision-making remains final

### Planned Legal Knowledge Base

- Constitution of India
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)
- Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)
- Relevant Central and State Acts
- Rules and Regulations
- Government Notifications
- Relevant Court Judgments
- Legal Amendments and Updates

### Platforms

- Web Application
- Mobile Application
- Responsive interface for computers and smartphones

### Important Notice

Intelligence Law is intended to provide legal information and decision-support. It does not replace a court, judge, lawyer, police authority, or other legally authorized institution.

Legal information must be verified against current and authoritative sources before being relied upon for important decisions.
